Key Moments That Shaped the Race

NOV 30, 2022
ChatGPT launches — the starting gun
OpenAI releases ChatGPT to the public. One million users in 5 days. 100 million users in 2 months. The fastest consumer adoption in internet history. No website had ever grown this fast.
MAR 2023
Google fires back with Bard (now Gemini)
Google's response was widely seen as rushed. Bard made a factual error in its first public demo, wiping $100B off Alphabet's market cap in a single day. But the traffic slowly built — and Bard was later rebuilt as Gemini with much stronger models.
MAR 2023
Anthropic launches Claude on claude.ai
Early traffic was modest — under 10M visits/month. But Claude quietly built a reputation for nuance, honesty, and safety. Enterprise adoption grew steadily throughout 2024. By early 2026, it had the highest year-over-year growth of any AI platform.
JAN 2025
DeepSeek shocks the world
Chinese AI lab DeepSeek released R1 — a model that matched or beat GPT-4 at a fraction of the training cost. The internet flooded to deepseek.com. Nvidia's stock dropped 17% in one day. The assumption that only US companies could lead in AI was suddenly in question.
MID 2025
Gemini crosses 1 billion monthly visits
Google's deep integration of Gemini into Search, Android, Gmail, and Google Workspace began paying off. Gemini grew 157% between April and September 2025, powered largely by India becoming its top market. ChatGPT's share fell from 87% to 65% in 12 months.
FEB–MAR 2026
Claude surges — the quiet winner
Claude.ai posted the single largest month-over-month gain of any website tracked by SimilarWeb in March 2026 — up 420% year-over-year. While ChatGPT dipped 6.5% in February 2026, Claude grew 43%. The enterprise and developer market was beginning to diversify away from a single AI provider.

What This Means for You

For finance and business professionals: The AI tool you use today may not be the best tool for your job in 12 months. ChatGPT is still dominant, but Gemini is deeply integrated into the Google Workspace tools most finance teams already use. Claude is earning a strong reputation in enterprise environments for accuracy and nuance — both critical in finance. Perplexity is becoming the default research tool for professionals who need cited, verifiable answers.

The real insight: This is not a winner-takes-all market like search was. Just as professionals today use different tools for different tasks (Excel for modelling, Power BI for reporting, Outlook for communication), we are moving toward a world where different AI tools serve different professional needs. The skill is learning which AI to use for which task — not just knowing one tool exists.

For your career: The professionals who will thrive in the next 5 years are not just those who know AI exists. They are the ones who understand the landscape, experiment with multiple tools, and can guide their teams and organisations in using them effectively. That expertise is rare — and valuable.
